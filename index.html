THE JOURNEY

A STORY WRITTEN THROUGH CHOICE

Dharmadevas Blade TxT is a text-based role-playing game where the world is presented through dialogue, decisions, exploration and combat.

Enter the world.

The adventure begins with a prologue that introduces the world and sets the foundation for the journey ahead.

From there, your decisions determine what happens next. Explore regions, encounter enemies, discover items, learn abilities and survive the battles ahead.

There is no giant open-world map hiding behind the interface. The world is delivered through text — allowing the story itself to remain at the center of the experience.
