// THE GAME

A JOURNEY
WRITTEN IN WORDS

Dharmadevas Blade is a story-focused text RPG where the world is presented through dialogue, choices, exploration and combat.

Instead of controlling a character through a three-dimensional world, you experience the journey directly through the game's text, choices and systems.

Travel through different regions, encounter enemies, manage your inventory, develop your character and make decisions as the story unfolds.

The TXT edition is the current playable form of the project — the foundation upon which the larger Dharmadevas Blade game will continue to grow.
